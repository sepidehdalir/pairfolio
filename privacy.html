PRIVACY POLICY

Your job stays
under your control.

Effective October 22, 2026 · Pairfolio1.0 · Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir

Local job processing

Pairfolio stores job details, selected photographs, task checkmarks, report styles and templates on your device. There is no Pairfolio account, developer-operated cloud, advertising SDK, tracking SDK or analytics uploader. The developer does not receive your jobs, photos or reports through the app.

Photos and camera

The system Photos picker shares only items you select. Camera access is requested when you choose to take a job photo. Pairfolio creates a bounded, oriented JPEG copy without original photo location metadata. Originals remain unchanged. Jobs use the date you enter; the app does not request location access or verify when and where a photograph was taken.

Purchases

Apple manages optional Lifetime Pro purchases, payment information and Apple Account authentication. Pairfolio uses StoreKit to check verified access and support restoration. The developer does not operate a purchase backend or receive payment-card details through the app.

Exports, sharing and backups

PDF reports and portable job backups are generated locally. You choose whether to save or share them, and the destination. Your chosen file provider, sharing service and operating-system backup settings have their own privacy practices. Reports and backups can contain client names, addresses, photographs and business details that you entered; review them before sharing.

Retention and removal

Jobs in recoverable Trash remain on the device until restored or the app is removed. There is no automatic purge. Replaced photo files may remain in local storage to protect previous work after interrupted saves. Removing the app removes its local jobs, templates and Trash. External exports and OS backups must be managed separately. Keep important job backups outside the app before removal.

Support and website

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send. Avoid sending client photographs or personal details unless needed for the issue. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard hosting requests under its own privacy policy. There is no custom analytics or advertising script on this site.

Contact and changes

Questions: hello@arvinify.com. Material changes to app data practices will be reflected in this policy and the applicable App Store declaration.
