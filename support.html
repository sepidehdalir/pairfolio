Start a job

Choose New job, enter a title and any client/site details, then Save job. Add a room or task and put a photograph in its Before and After slot. Take a photo or choose one from Photos. A cloud-only original may need to download before import.

Check the details

Inspect each photograph, correct a reversed label with Swap and add factual notes. Task marked done is your checklist status. Photo documentation is checked separately. A task outside the job's scope needs a reason. Missing photos remain visible, and an incomplete PDF is labeled Draft.

Make a report

Choose US Letter or A4, then Preview client PDF. Check every page, then use Save or share PDF to save to Files, AirDrop or another destination offered by your device. Images are fitted without cropping. Reports do not certify work quality, authenticity or customer acceptance.

Back up and restore

Back up this job creates a portable .pairfolio file containing that job, its photos and report style. Save it outside the app. In More options, choose Import job backup, or open the backup file with Pairfolio. Import creates a fresh job and does not replace an existing one. Free admission allows one active job; move the existing one to recoverable Trash first if needed.

Recoverable Trash

Moving a job to Trash preserves it on the device. Open Recoverable Trash and choose Restore job. Restoring checks the active-job limit. Nothing is automatically purged. Removing the app removes its local jobs and Trash, so keep external backups.

Lifetime Pro

The free workflow includes one active job, three pairs, editing/checklist, PDF and backup. Optional Lifetime Pro adds up to 100 active jobs, 60 pairs per job, your logo and 10 reusable report templates. The price is shown from Apple in the app. Use Restore Purchases for existing access; this does not restore job data. Existing saved work remains editable and exportable if Pro is unavailable.

If a save or import fails

Keep the editor open and retry after checking available storage. A failed job save preserves the previous saved version; unreadable files are not silently replaced. An invalid, unsupported or damaged backup is rejected. Preserve the original backup file and contact support with the exact message. Never send credentials.
